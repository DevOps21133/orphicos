Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

The short version

OrphicOS is deployed and runs on infrastructure you own or control. The workflows it performs, the screen data it reads, the documents and systems it touches, and the AI engine itself all stay on your machines — they are processed locally, without needing an internet connection, and are never sent to us. The only personal data we handle is what you send us directly: for example, the contact details you provide when you book a consultation or correspond with us.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for the processing described here is the operator of OrphicOS, reachable at hello@orphicos.app. Full contact details are on the imprint page. For data that OrphicOS processes while running inside your organisation, your organisation is the controller.

2. What we process, and why

Consultation and contact data. When you email us or request a consultation, we process the details you choose to share — such as your name, email address, company, and the content of your message — to respond and to scope a possible engagement. Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to a contract, and our legitimate interest in business correspondence (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Kept for as long as needed for the business relationship.

Data OrphicOS processes on your infrastructure. When OrphicOS runs, it operates on machines you own or control. The commands it carries out, the on-screen data it reads, the files and applications it works in, and the AI models it uses all remain within your own environment. OrphicOS does not require an internet connection to perform this work, and we do not receive, transmit, or store this operational data. Within your organisation you are the controller of it, and it is governed by your own policies and your deployment agreement with us.

Support data. If you ask us for support, we process the information you choose to share with us for that purpose. We do not require, and do not ask for, your operational data to provide it.

Website. This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking of any kind, and serves all fonts and assets from our own servers — it makes no third-party requests.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not receive or store the data OrphicOS processes on your machines — it never leaves your infrastructure.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • All fonts and assets on this site are served from our own servers — no third-party requests.

4. Recipients and transfers

For this website and our correspondence with you, we use standard hosting and email providers, which process data only as needed to provide those functions and are bound by data-processing agreements. Because the operational data handled by OrphicOS stays on your own infrastructure, it is not disclosed to us or to any subprocessor. Where a provider used for correspondence processes data in the United States, transfers rely on recognised safeguards under Chapter V GDPR. All transmission to and from this site is encrypted in transit (TLS).

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights in respect of the data we hold — for example your consultation correspondence — email hello@orphicos.app.

6. Security

All traffic between this site and your browser is encrypted with TLS. The design principle behind the product is that the most sensitive data — the work OrphicOS does and everything it reads to do it — never leaves your own security perimeter, because it is processed on your infrastructure rather than ours.

7. Changes

We will update this policy as the product evolves and change the date above. Material changes will be reflected here.
