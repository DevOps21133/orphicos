Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

The short version

To carry out a command, OrphicOS sends your command and a compact, text-based map of your current window to OrphicOS servers, where the next action is decided. That screen data is held in memory for the single decision and then discarded — it is never written to disk, never logged, and never stored. Voice audio never leaves your machine at all: it is transcribed on your device, and only the resulting text is sent, after you have read and confirmed it.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for data processing described here is the operator of OrphicOS, reachable at hello@orphicos.app. Full contact details are on the imprint page.

2. What we process, and why

Account data. When you register we store your email address and a salted, one-way hash of your password (we cannot read your password). Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Kept until you delete your account.

Commands and screen data. When you submit a command, the app sends the command text and a compact description of your active window (element names, roles and states — not a recording of your screen) to OrphicOS servers, where the next action for your machine is decided. In rare cases where that description is insufficient, a single screenshot of the current screen is sent for the same purpose. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Retention: none. Screen data and screenshots exist in server memory only for the duration of the individual decision and are then discarded.

Voice. Audio from your microphone is transcribed into text locally on your device. The audio itself is never transmitted anywhere. The transcribed text is only sent if you confirm it.

Usage metadata. We record technical metadata about requests — timestamps, action types, latency and volume counters per account — to operate, meter and secure the service. This metadata contains no screen contents. Legal basis: performance of a contract and legitimate interest in service security (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Server logs. Our web servers are configured not to keep access logs for application traffic. Standard, short-lived technical logs may exist for security and fault diagnosis (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. What we do not do

  • We do not store your screen data, screenshots, or the contents of your files.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • All fonts and assets on this site are served from our own servers — no third-party requests.

4. Recipients and transfers

We use hosting infrastructure providers to run OrphicOS servers, and specialised AI infrastructure subprocessors to compute the decision for each command. These providers process data only as needed to provide the service and are bound by data-processing agreements. Some subprocessors may process data in the United States; where that is the case, transfers rely on recognised safeguards under Chapter V GDPR. All transmission is encrypted in transit (TLS).

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to processing based on legitimate interests, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights — including deleting your account and all associated data — email hello@orphicos.app.

6. Security

All traffic between the app, this site and OrphicOS servers is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2 hashes. Access tokens are stored hashed on the server. The design principle behind the service is data minimisation: the most sensitive data — your screen — is engineered to be unstorable, not merely unstored.

7. Changes

We will update this policy as the service evolves and change the date above. Material changes affecting registered users will be announced by email.
