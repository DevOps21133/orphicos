OrphicOS

the machine works. You don’t.

Run your PC by voice or text

You speak or type. Your machine does it. Open apps, write emails, control your software, browse, move files — hands-free. OrphicOS reads what’s on your screen, decides the next step, and carries it out. You watch every action as it happens, and one key stops everything.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install

    A small Windows app. No model setup, no keys, no configuration.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    Your OrphicOS account connects your machine to the OrphicOS engine.

  3. 3

    Speak

    Hold the push-to-talk key and say what you want done. Or just type it.

Your data

Processed securely on OrphicOS servers. Encrypted in transit. Your screen data is never stored.

Voice audio is transcribed on your device.
