The machine
works.
You don’t.

Run your Windows 11 PC by voice or text.

You speak or type. Your machine does it. Open apps, write emails, control your software, browse, move files — hands-free. OrphicOS reads what’s on your screen, decides the next step, and carries it out. You watch every action, and one key stops everything.

  1. Install.

    A small Windows app. No model setup, no keys, no configuration.

  2. Sign in.

    Your OrphicOS account connects your machine to the OrphicOS engine.

  3. Speak.

    Hold the push-to-talk key and say what you want done. Or type it.

Processed securely on OrphicOS servers. Encrypted in transit. Your screen data is never stored.

Voice audio is transcribed on your device.
