The machine works.
You don’t.

Run your PC by voice or text. You say it; your machine does it.

orphicos — session
> open notepad and type: the machine works.

  reading screen   ui tree
  launch           notepad.exe
  focus            Untitled — Notepad
  type             "the machine works."

  done
>

A real command from our test runs. Open apps, write emails, control software, browse, move files — hands-free. You watch every step; one key stops everything.

01Install · Sign in · Speak

  1. install

    A small Windows app. No model setup, no keys, no configuration.

  2. sign in

    Your OrphicOS account connects your machine to the OrphicOS engine.

  3. speak

    Hold the push-to-talk key and say what you want done. Or type it.

02Your data

Processed securely on OrphicOS servers. Encrypted in transit. Your screen data is never stored.

Voice audio is transcribed on your device.
